FAQ

Fair questions.

Where do the leads come from?

Public sources, worked hard: local business press, franchise-development pages and their published site criteria, state franchise wires, brand location maps, trade-area sweeps, civic and planning signals. Every lead in your report links to its source. What we don't do: scrape behind logins, buy sketchy lists, or invent "proprietary data."

Does Spector email prospects for me?

No — and that's a feature. Drafts arrive pre-filled so one click opens them in your mail client, from your address. The prospect hears from a real broker, the relationship lands with you, and nothing goes out without your approval. Ever.

What kind of listings does this work for?

Retail, restaurant, service, medical/wellness and small-format office/flex — anywhere the winning play is "find the expanding operator whose footprint fits." Large industrial or single-tenant net-lease deals need a different toolkit; ask us first and we'll tell you honestly.

What's honestly NOT in the report?

Lease-expiration data — the biggest prospect pool never announces itself, and it lives in CoStar and your tenant-rep network, not public sources. We say so in every report instead of faking it. Spector complements your data; it doesn't impersonate it.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes — the taste is free and it's not a demo. Download the full sample report above: it's the real deliverable from a real Colorado listing, shared with permission. That exact depth is what your property gets at onboarding. If your first report doesn't help you, we refund the month — no questions.

Why per person and not per property?

Because brokers told us so. A working broker carries several listings at once — metering each one punishes exactly the people the desk is for. One membership, your whole book: every property gets the full onboarding report, the living target list, and outreach drafts. Our scoreboard metric is still days-to-filled, and every signed lease still earns the commemorative Vacancy Filled 🎉 Receipt.

You, personally — unlimited properties: full onboarding reports, prospecting passes every business day across your active listings, living target lists, queued outreach drafts, the dedicated inbox, the Monday digest, and the live chat desk on this page. Team of brokers? One desk per person keeps every member's book private. Truly enormous portfolios: write the desk and we'll talk honestly.
